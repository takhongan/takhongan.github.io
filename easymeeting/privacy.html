Privacy Policy

[Collection of User Information]

This app sends audio and text data entered or recorded by users to external AI services for the purpose of providing meeting minutes generation functionality.

[Use of User Information]

The transmitted data is used solely for summarization, analysis, and the generation of meeting minutes.

Such data is not stored on this app’s servers. This app does not collect personally identifiable information (such as name, address, or email address).

In addition, this app does not track user behavior.

[Provision of User Information to Third Parties]

This app uses APIs provided by OpenAI as external services.

For information regarding the privacy practices of external services, please refer to the privacy policies of the respective service providers.
